You might be wondering: how does TrustLayer AI actually detect hallucinations?

We use eight different detection techniques, running in parallel for speed and accuracy.

Self-Consistency Checking asks the same question multiple ways. If answers contradict each other, that's a red flag.

Source Verification checks if citations, references, and URLs actually exist.

And Enterprise Grounding — this is where we really differentiate — we connect directly to your authoritative data sources. Your CRM. Your product catalog. Your policy databases. When the AI claims something about your business, we verify it against the truth.
